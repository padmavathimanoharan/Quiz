# Quiz
Introducing QuizMaster: Your Ultimate iOS Quiz App!

📚 Are you ready to challenge your knowledge and have some fun along the way? QuizMaster is the perfect app to test your wits and learn something new! Whether you're a trivia enthusiast or just looking for a casual brain teaser, QuizMaster has you covered.

🏠 **Home Page**: Start your quiz journey on the Home Page. Here, you'll find a user-friendly interface that welcomes you to the world of exciting quizzes. Choose from various quiz categories and let the adventure begin!

📜 **Instructions**: Before you dive into the quiz, we provide clear and concise instructions to guide you through the game. Whether you're a first-time player or a seasoned pro, our instructions ensure a seamless and enjoyable experience.

❓ **Questions**: Get ready to put your knowledge to the test with our thoughtfully crafted questions. We cover a wide range of topics, from history and science to pop culture and sports. With QuizMaster, you'll never run out of intriguing challenges.

📝 **Option Selection Page**: Make your selections and submit your answers on this interactive page. We provide multiple-choice options, making it easy for you to pick the correct answer. If you're unsure, don't worry – take your best guess, and you might be surprised!

📊 **Results Summary**: After answering all the questions, your performance will be summarized in an easy-to-read format. You'll see how many questions you got right, your total score, and a breakdown of your answers. Challenge yourself to do better next time or share your results with friends to compare scores!
